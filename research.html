Doctoral Research

PhD Topic: International Corporate Governance in the Middle East

London South Bank University (LSBU) · 2022 – Present · Supervisor: LSBU Faculty of Business, Law and Computing

The doctoral research investigates the development and effectiveness of corporate governance frameworks in the Middle East, with particular focus on the financial sector in the UAE and Qatar. It examines how regulatory bodies — including the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) and the Qatar Financial Centre Regulatory Authority (QFCRA) — have designed and implemented governance standards, and how these align with international best practice and ESG criteria.

The research adopts a comparative law and regulatory theory methodology, drawing on legal analysis, policy documents, and empirical data to understand how governance quality affects corporate behaviour, sustainability outcomes, and economic development in emerging markets.

Key questions:

  • How effective are current corporate governance regulations for banks in the UAE and Qatar?
  • How do governance frameworks interact with ESG imperatives and sustainability commitments?
  • What lessons can be drawn for governance reform in other emerging-market jurisdictions?

Research Themes

⚖️ Corporate Governance & Financial Regulation

Analysis of governance codes, board structures, and regulatory compliance frameworks for banks and financial institutions, with a comparative focus on the UAE, Qatar, and the UK. Examines how governance quality shapes risk management, accountability, and long-term value creation in the financial sector.

Board Governance Banking Regulation Regulatory Compliance GCC Financial Markets

🌿 ESG & Sustainable Finance

Research on the integration of environmental, social, and governance (ESG) factors into financial regulation and corporate strategy. Focuses on how ESG frameworks can be embedded in governance codes and how they relate to the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

ESG Integration SDG Alignment Green Finance Responsible Investment

🏗️ CCUS & Climate Policy

Research on carbon capture, utilisation, and storage (CCUS) technology and policy, with particular focus on the UAE's CCUS strategy and Net Zero 2050 commitments. Examines the role of policy, investment, and international cooperation in scaling CCUS deployment.

CCUS Technology Net Zero Policy UAE Climate Strategy COP28 Legacy

🔄 Offshore Circular Economy

Investigation of circular economy principles applied to the offshore energy sector in the Middle East. Examines how advanced digital technologies (AI, IoT, optimisation algorithms) can enable sustainable end-of-life asset management and reduce the environmental impact of offshore decommissioning.

Circular Economy Offshore Decommissioning Digital Technologies Steel Recycling

🏦 Private Equity & M&A

Research drawing on professional practice in M&A and private equity transactions, examining governance implications of deal structuring, ownership concentration, and post-acquisition integration in emerging markets.

Private Equity M&A Deal Governance Capital Structuring

🌐 Comparative Law

Comparative analysis of corporate governance and financial regulation across jurisdictions, including Brazil, the UK, UAE, Qatar, and the broader GCC, identifying convergences and divergences in regulatory approach and policy outcomes.

Brazil United Kingdom UAE & Qatar GCC Region
