Welcome

I am a corporate lawyer qualified in Brazil and a governance and compliance professional qualified by the UK Chartered Governance Institute. I am certified in Responsible AI by the Oxford AI Ethics, Regulation and Compliance Programme at Saïd Business School, University of Oxford, and certified in U.S. export controls by the World Trade Center Denver.

I am currently a PhD candidate at London South Bank University (LSBU), where my research focuses on the contribution of joint ventures to the energy transition.

With over twenty years of professional experience across Brazil, the United Kingdom, and the Middle East, I bring an interdisciplinary perspective that bridges legal scholarship, regulatory analysis, and professional practice. My work draws on experience in banking law at HSBC, private equity, capital markets, and large-scale joint venture environments to examine how governance frameworks shape sustainable development outcomes.

This site presents my academic publications, professional background, and ongoing research projects. I welcome enquiries from researchers, policymakers, and professional collaborators.
