Welcome

I am a corporate lawyer and PhD candidate at London South Bank University (LSBU), where my research focuses on international corporate governance, ESG frameworks, and sustainability regulation in the Middle East and global financial markets.

With over fifteen years of practice across Brazil, the United Kingdom, and international markets, I bring an interdisciplinary perspective that bridges legal scholarship, regulatory analysis, and professional practice. My work draws on experience in banking law at HSBC, private equity, and capital markets to examine how governance frameworks shape sustainable development outcomes.

This site presents my academic publications, professional background, and ongoing research projects. I welcome enquiries from researchers, policymakers, and professional collaborators.
