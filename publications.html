Book Chapters

Book Chapter

"CCUS in the UAE"

Villasuso Lago, F.

In: Pereira, E. G. et al. (Eds.), Carbon Capture Utilization and Storage, Sustainable Development Goals Series (SDGS). Springer Nature, 2025. Chapter 20, pp. 515–533.

This chapter provides a comprehensive overview of the UAE's carbon capture, utilisation, and storage (CCUS) projects, regulatory framework, and climate strategy. It situates the UAE's progress in the context of its hosting of COP28 and its Net Zero by 2050 initiative, examining both current practice and further policy steps needed for broader CCUS adoption.

Conference Papers

Conference Paper

"Advancing Sustainable Offshore Circular Economy in the Middle East: Pathways to Net-Zero and Role of Advanced Digital Technologies"

Qi, C.; Suboyin, A.; Baobaid, O.; Rahman, M. M.; Thomsen, J.; Diaz, S.; Villasuso Lago, F.

ASME 2025 44th International Conference on Ocean, Offshore and Arctic Engineering (OMAE2025). Proceedings Volume V006T11A072. Published 22 June 2025.

This paper examines the transition from traditional offshore asset disposal methods to a circular economy model in the Middle East, leveraging advanced digital technologies including AI, optimisation algorithms, and IoT. Drawing on case studies from the UK North Sea and the EU, it demonstrates the feasibility and environmental benefits of circular approaches for offshore decommissioning, arguing for adoption in the Middle East to improve ESG compliance and advance net-zero ambitions.

Policy Articles & Regulatory Analyses

Policy Article

"UAE: Central Bank Corporate Governance Regulations"

Villasuso Lago, F.

Independent Research. Available via ResearchGate and Academia.edu.

An analytical commentary on the Corporate Governance Regulations introduced by the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE), published in the official gazette on 15 August 2019 and entering into effect on 15 September 2019. The analysis covers the regulatory requirements applicable to all CBUAE-licensed banks, including subsidiaries, affiliates, and international branches, with particular attention to board responsibilities, governance policies, risk management frameworks, and Islamic financial services requirements.

Policy Article

"Qatar: Corporate Governance – Qatar Financial Centre Regulatory Authority (QFCRA) Governance and Controlled Functions Rules 2020"

Villasuso Lago, F.

Independent Research. Available via ResearchGate and Academia.edu.

An analytical overview of the QFCRA's Governance and Controlled Functions Rules 2020 and associated disciplinary measures, examining how Qatar's regulatory authority has sought to enhance corporate governance standards for financial institutions operating within the Qatar Financial Centre. The paper considers the regulatory framework's alignment with international best practices and identifies key implications for governance reform in Qatar and the broader GCC region.
