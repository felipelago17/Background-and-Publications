Biography

Felipe is a corporate lawyer qualified in Brazil and a governance and compliance professional qualified by the UK Chartered Governance Institute. He is certified in Responsible AI through the Oxford AI Ethics, Regulation and Compliance Programme at Saïd Business School, University of Oxford, and in U.S. export controls by the World Trade Center Denver.

Since 2022, he has been enrolled as a PhD researcher investigating the contribution of joint ventures to the energy transition, with a comparative focus on Norway and the United Arab Emirates. His research examines the energy governance frameworks and institutional policies shaping joint venture development.
