Biography

Felipe Villasuso Lago is a corporate lawyer and academic researcher with over fifteen years of professional experience in corporate and banking law, private equity, and international capital markets, spanning Brazil and the United Kingdom.

Currently a PhD candidate at London South Bank University (LSBU) since September 2022, his doctoral research focuses on corporate governance with a particular emphasis on regulatory frameworks in the Middle East, ESG, and sustainability policy. His interdisciplinary approach combines legal analysis, regulatory theory, and empirical research to understand how governance structures influence sustainable development outcomes.

A member of the Brazilian Bar Association (OAB), Felipe holds dual Brazilian and Spanish citizenship and has practised law in Brazil and the United Kingdom. His legal career has spanned corporate secretarial work at HSBC Investment Bank Holdings Limited in London, and advisory roles at HSBC Bank Brasil S.A., where he specialised in corporate law, banking regulation, and capital markets transactions.

His academic contributions include a book chapter published by Springer Nature and a peer-reviewed conference paper presented at the ASME 44th International Conference on Ocean, Offshore and Arctic Engineering (OMAE 2025). His work is aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, particularly SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth) and SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production).
